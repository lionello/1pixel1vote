# 1pixel1vote

## Visual hierarchy

The election picker separates general project information from the selected election's content.

- Keep the site-wide header and sidebar content above the picker general to 1pixel1vote. Do not put an election's name, dates, status, vote totals, or other election-specific details there.
- Place election-specific information and controls below the picker in the sidebar, or within a map or other area clearly scoped to the selected election. Their content must match the selected election.
- British Columbia's 2026 provincial election is the first dataset, not the identity or scope of the project. Preserve this hierarchy as other elections are added.
